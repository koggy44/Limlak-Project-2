I have uploaded a brochure for a SACCO (Savings and Credit Cooperative). Please analyze the contents, branding, core offerings, and message in the document, and build a full-stack, responsive SACCO website based on it.

### General Design & Requirements
1. Tech Stack: Use React (or Vite + React) with Tailwind CSS for clean, professional UI styling, along with Lucide React icons.
2. Aesthetic: Modern, trustworthy, secure, and accessible. Use the color palette and logo concepts from the brochure.
3. Architecture: Implement smooth single-page navigation (or multi-page routing using React Router) with a unified Header/Navbar and Footer.
4. Call-to-Actions (CTAs): Include prominent "Join SACCO / Become a Member" and "Member Login" buttons throughout the site.

---

### Detailed Page Structure & Requirements

1. Homepage (`/` or Home Section)
- Hero Banner: High-impact headline derived from the brochure's tagline, dynamic background/imagery, and quick links to "Join Now" or "Calculate Loan."
- Quick Stats/Trust Metrics: Key numbers (e.g., active members, total assets, years of operation).
- Key Value Highlights: Overview of competitive interest rates, fast loan approvals, and member dividends.
- Featured Products Teaser: Snapshots of popular savings and loan products.
- Interactive Loan & Savings Calculator: A functional widget allowing users to estimate monthly loan repayments or savings growth based on duration and amount.
- Testimonials & News Feed: Member success stories and recent SACCO announcements.

2. About Us (`/about`)
- Mission, Vision, and Core Values: Extracted directly from the brochure.
- Our History / Journey: Timeline or background narrative of the SACCO.
- Leadership & Governance: Grid showing Board of Directors and Executive Management (use placeholder avatars if photos aren't in the brochure).
- Financial Strength & Compliance: Brief statement on regulatory compliance (e.g., SASRA or local regulatory body adherence).

3. Products & Services (`/products`)
- Categorized Offerings:
  - Savings & Deposits: Share Capital, Normal Savings, Fixed Deposits, Junior/Holiday Savings.
  - Credit & Loans: Emergency Loans, Development/Asset Loans, Instant Mobile Loans, Mortgage/Land Loans.
  - Member Benefits: Dividend history overview, insurance cover, micro-investment options.
- Detailed Product Cards: Each showing eligibility criteria, maximum amounts, repayment terms, and interest rates.
- "Apply Now" Trigger: Modal or direct link from each product card to a membership/loan application form.

4. Membership (`/membership`)
- How to Join: Step-by-step breakdown of eligibility and registration steps.
- Requirements Checklist: List of required documents (ID, passport photo, KRA PIN/tax ID, registration fee).
- Downloadable Resources: Section for PDFs (e.g., Membership Form, By-laws, Financial Reports).
- Digital Application Form: Interactive form collecting personal details, next of kin, and desired savings plan.

5. Careers (`/careers`)
- Culture & Life at SACCO: Short introduction on working conditions and corporate environment.
- Current Job Openings: Accordion or list of open positions with expandable job descriptions, qualifications, and application deadlines.
- Resume Submission Form: Quick application form allowing applicants to input their details and select the target role.

6. Contact Us (`/contact`)
- Contact Info: Physical office address, main telephone numbers, support emails, and operating hours.
- Interactive Map Component: Visual location map placeholder.
- Branch / ATM Locator: List or dropdown of physical branch offices with specific phone numbers.
- Inquiries Form: Name, email, phone, subject, and message input fields with client-side validation.

7. FAQs & Help Center (`/faq`)
- Searchable or tabbed FAQ section covering common questions: Joining requirements, Loan eligibility, Dividend payouts, and Online banking access.
